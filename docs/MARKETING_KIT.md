# Explore the Universe 2175 — Marketing Kit

Telford Projects presents. Every claim below was checked against the game
repository (`etelford32/ETU`) in October 2026. The "Claims to avoid" section
lists what the code does **not** support yet; keep it in mind for every
trailer card, store blurb and post.

---

## Positioning

**Explore the Universe 2175 is an open-galaxy space adventure.** Every run
generates a new spiral galaxy full of quests, enemies, bosses and real
celestial phenomena, and you explore it with Cyl, a crystal AI companion who
learns who you are.

- **Genre line:** Open-galaxy space action-RPG
- **Players:** Solo, or co-op for 2–4 pilots on Steam
- **Platforms:** PC (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- **Status:** Free Steam Playtest open now. Early Access 2027.

### The four pillars

1. **A new galaxy every run.** Spiral arms, nebulae, pulsars, star clusters
   and thousands of planets and moons, generated fresh from a seed. Sixteen
   themed regions, from fungal jungles to the frozen Outer Rim.
2. **Real cosmic danger.** Slingshot around black holes. Outrun solar flares
   and coronal mass ejections modeled on real observations. No two black
   holes look or behave the same.
3. **Quests and a buried mystery.** Wake ancient stations, earn the trust of
   dwarves, bear kings and fungal queens, and follow the Khepri Signal to learn
   why the Lumari were erased.
4. **Bosses built to be broken.** Strip MEGABOT's armour plate by plate,
   escape the Null Architect's prison rooms, and outlast a new boss every few
   minutes.

## Steam "About This Game"

> **A new galaxy every run.** Explore the Universe 2175 drops you into a
> freshly generated spiral galaxy: hundreds of star systems, nebulae, pulsars
> and black holes, and thousands of planets and moons across sixteen themed
> regions. Chart it, mine it, and find out what's waiting at the edge of the
> map.
>
> **Real cosmic danger.** Space here is beautiful and it wants you dead.
> Black holes pull on you through real gravity wells; skim one and slingshot
> out faster than you went in. Stars flare and throw coronal mass ejections
> modeled on real observations. Comets burn, asteroid fields crowd, and every
> black hole is its own creature.
>
> **Fly with Cyl.** Cyl is the last of the Lumari, a crystal intelligence
> riding in your ship. She fights beside you, heals you, calls the space
> weather and teaches you the astronomy behind it. She asks who you are and
> remembers your answers, growing into a warrior, a caregiver, a lover or a
> seeker depending on you.
>
> **Quests and a buried mystery.** Wake ancient stations and link them into a
> network. Trade with nervous merchants, earn guild trust from a dwarf king
> "in crystal fire," carry messages between a fungal queen and a miner, and
> follow the Khepri Signal toward the truth: the Lumari did not crash. They
> were executed.
>
> **Bosses built to be broken.** The Mega Mecha Scout counts its missiles out
> loud. The Alpha Robot fights beside a shield with a mind of its own. The
> Null Architect builds prison rooms around you and closes a holographic cube
> you must break your way out of. And MEGABOT, twenty-five ships tall, wears
> fifteen plates of armour you strip with missiles before you burn the
> exposed servos with lasers. He remembers what you did to his empire, and he
> decides how to come for you.
>
> **Build your pilot.** Earn XP, spend skill points, and commit to a doctrine
> — Quantum Lattice, Plasma Furnace or Event Horizon — that reshapes your
> lasers, missiles, mines, shields and thrusters together. Craft, trade,
> collect glowing loot and grow a base of awakened stations.
>
> **Talk to anyone.** Type to any named pilot, boss or faction leader and get
> an answer in their own voice. They remember you next run.
>
> **Bring friends.** Co-op Survival for two to four pilots on Steam, sharing
> one galaxy.

## Claims to avoid (not in the game yet)

- **"An enemy AI that learns your tactics."** MEGABOT escalates from what you
  did to his empire during the run and enemies track who hurt them, but no
  enemy learns your fighting style. Say instead: *"MEGABOT remembers what you
  did to his empire."* The thing that truly learns you is **Cyl**.
- **Wormhole travel.** There is one wormhole object, but no travel.
- **Quasars, magnetars, white dwarfs, supernova explosions, star formation.**
  Planned or mentioned only.
- **Relativistic effects / time dilation, real gravitational lensing.**
  Lensing is an artistic approximation.
- **A body-splitting MEGABOT phase, or co-op MEGABOT.** Planned.
- **Campaign mode, PvP, a full faction alliance arc, defense trees beyond
  Shields, permanent laser mastery.** Planned.
- **"Roguelike space RTS" / "17 factions at war."** The game is an action-RPG
  adventure; 18 factions exist in the lore, four are live on the site.
